R/01
Mechanistic Interpretability · Co-author

Hyperbolic Geometry of Reasoning in LLM Hidden States

Submitted — Mechanistic Interpretability Workshop @ ICML 2026 (under review)

Implemented a Poincaré-ball hyperbolic probe (spectrally-normalised projection, learnable bounded scaling, exponential map, Maximum Distance Rescaling) over the hidden states of a 7B-parameter model. The probe recovered reasoning depth at Spearman ρ = 0.967, where a matched Euclidean probe collapsed to ρ = 0.488 — a geometric diagnostic of how chain-of-thought distillation reshapes hidden-state structure.

Clinical Video Analytics · Research → B.Tech Project

Weak-Signal Motion Detection in Patient Video

Neuro Computing Lab, IIT Delhi · Prof. T. K. Gandhi & Dr. M. Bhatia

A detection pipeline for sub-pixel involuntary movements in long-duration patient monitoring video, where the target signal is buried in sensor noise. Built on de-noised optical flow, adaptive temporal smoothing, and dynamic thresholding — a 40%+ improvement in subtle-motion detection over baseline. Now being compressed for edge deployment on low-power embedded hardware.
